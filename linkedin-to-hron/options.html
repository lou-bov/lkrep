LinkedIn → HR-ON settings

Set this to the URL of HR-ON's "Create candidate" page to go there directly. Comma-separated. Pre-filled for every new candidate; you can change them per candidate. Only needed if a field isn't found automatically. Right-click the field in HR-ON → Inspect, and use e.g. its name. Keys: firstName, lastName, email, phone, linkedinUrl, headline, company, location, notes, job, tags, cv.
